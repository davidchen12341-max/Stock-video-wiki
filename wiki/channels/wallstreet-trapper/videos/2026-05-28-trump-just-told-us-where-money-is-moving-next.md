# Trump Just Told Us Where Money Is Moving Next

**Thesis:** Quantum computing is the next major investment wave — Trump publicly said "quantum," which Trapper reads as the government validation signal that precedes institutional positioning. Three ETFs give exposure to the full spectrum from infrastructure to pure-play to national security, without having to pick the single winning company.

**Key takeaways:**

- **The signal:** Trump said "quantum" publicly (video opens with clip of Trump at presidential podium). Per Trapper's framework: government money validates importance → institutions build positions → ETFs load up → public catches on late. "Once government backs a sector the way they've been doing, Wall Street starts building narratives around it." Same pattern preceded AI, semiconductors, and defense tech.
- **Why quantum matters:** Traditional computing hits limits (power, heat, processing). Quantum solves problems classical systems can't — drug discovery, military simulation, cybersecurity, encryption. "This is not just a technology race. It's a national security race."
- **ETF #1 — QTUM (Defiance Quantum ETF):** Infrastructure/ecosystem approach. ~83% tech. Top holdings confirmed in video frame: MU (2.96%), INTC (2.64%), STM (2.34%), NOK (2.30%), Global Unichip (2.12%), ON (2.01%), TSEM (1.99%), AMD (1.98%), ARM (1.98%). "Don't need to know the exact winner — own the stack."
- **ETF #2 — Pure-play quantum ETF:** More aggressive, "own the frontier." Holdings confirmed in frame: RGTI Rigetti (6.49%), D-Wave DWAV (6.31%), IONQ IonQ (5.55%), QCI Quantum Computing (3.28%), IBM (3.10%), MSFT (2.74%). If quantum adoption accelerates, "these are the names that move the hardest."
- **ETF #3 — CQTM (Corgi Quantum Computing ETF):** $31.48, up +15.06% YTD (confirmed in video). Cybersecurity, encryption, government systems. "CQTM is not just betting on technology growth — it's betting on geopolitical importance." Government money flows here regardless of commercial quantum timeline because quantum threatens current encryption.

**Tickers mentioned:** QTUM, CQTM, INTC, MU, NOK, AMD, ARM, STM, ON, TSEM, RGTI, DWAV, IONQ, IBM, MSFT (plus Xanadu, Infleqtion, Global Unichip, Fujitsu, Mediatek — smaller weights in the ETFs)
