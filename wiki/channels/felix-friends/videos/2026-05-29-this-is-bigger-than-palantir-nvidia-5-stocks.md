# This is Bigger Than Palantir & Nvidia. These 5 Stocks Win the Next AI Boom

**Thesis:** AI's real bottleneck is now POWER, not chips. US data center demand 4x's in 4 years and only nuclear can fill it on a 24/7 basis. Felix presents 5 stocks covering every layer of the AI nuclear power value chain — plus a timing framework built specifically to avoid repeating the 2025 crash-and-hold mistake.

**Key takeaways:**

- **The bottleneck framework:** Step 1 = identify the bottleneck (AI's is now power). Step 2 = own the full value chain across all 5 layers. Step 3 = timing (buy only in the "climbing" phase / Pattern 2 — never during collapse). "Stocks change. Frameworks don't."
- **The 2025 lesson:** Oklo, NuScale, NaNuclear all had correct theses but retail investors bought the top and held through 65–80% collapses. Those same stocks are now basing again — a potential reset opportunity.
- **NNE (NaNuclear) — Layer 1, fuel logistics:** Acquired Secure Transportation Services for $13M — the company that physically moves nuclear fuel. "Bought the railway in the gold rush." $550M cash, zero revenue. Chart basing. Watch for Pattern 2 entry.
- **OKLO (Oklo) — Layer 2, reactor design:** DOE selected for surplus plutonium program (government gives them free fuel). Meta, Nvidia, Equinix as customers. $2.5B cash, pre-revenue. Test reactor July 2026; commercial 2032-2033. Base pattern forming.
- **SMR (NuScale Power) — Layer 3, licensed builder:** Only US NRC-certified SMR design — a regulatory moat that takes years and hundreds of millions to replicate. TVA + Romanian customers. Down ~80% from top; Fluor Corp (largest shareholder) sold out, clearing supply overhang. Still in gentle downtrend — watchlist only.
- **VICR (Vicor / "VCore") — Layer 4, power conversion:** Tiny chips that convert wall electricity to exact GPU voltage. No chip runs without one. **Profitable today.** Revenue guidance raised. New licensing deal. Near all-time highs — "boring is often where the smart money sits."
- **VRT (Vertiv) — Layer 5, data center infra:** Cooling, power distribution, thermal management for AI data centers. "What Cisco was to the internet boom." Customers: Microsoft, Amazon, Google, Meta — agnostic to who wins AI race. Profitable + free cash flow. Near all-time highs.
